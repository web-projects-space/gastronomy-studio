Korean Corn Dogs with Stretchy Cheese

⏱ 25 minutes 🍽 4 pieces Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆

A legendary Asian street food: juicy sausages and a stretchy block of mozzarella in a fluffy yeast batter, deep-fried in a super-crispy panko crust.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 sausages and 150 g mozzarella (in a firm block)
  • 160 g wheat flour
  • 1 tsp dry yeast
  • 1 tbsp sugar and 0.5 tsp salt
  • 120 ml warm water
  • 100 g panko breadcrumbs (flakes)
  • 1 medium potato, finely diced (optional)
  • Wooden skewers for frying
  • Vegetable oil for deep-frying
  • Ketchup, mayonnaise, or mustard for serving

Chef's Tips

✦ After skewering the cheese and sausages, chill them in the freezer for 10–15 minutes. That keeps the mozzarella from leaking out prematurely while frying in hot oil.

✦ The batter should be very stretchy and sticky. Wrap it around the filling with wet hands or by turning the skewer inside a tall container.

✦ Right after deep-frying, lightly dust the hot corn dog with a pinch of sugar, then drizzle ketchup and mustard in a zigzag. That sweet-and-salty contrast delivers the signature flavor!

✦ Don't hesitate to use raw potato—dice it very finely (3–4 mm cubes). In the hot oil, it cooks through and turns into a crispy crust over the batter.

COOKING

  1. 1

    Make the batter: mix the warm water, yeast, sugar, salt, and flour. Cover the bowl and leave in a warm spot for 30–45 minutes until the batter rises and becomes stretchy.

  2. 2

    Cut each sausage crosswise in half (giving 4 pieces). Cut the mozzarella into firm blocks the same size as the sausage halves. Skewer the filling onto wooden skewers (for a classic version, use a whole block of cheese or sausage; for the popular combo, thread half a sausage and a block of cheese onto each skewer).

  3. 3

    Dip each skewered piece into the stretchy batter, coating the filling completely. Want to recreate the famous Korean hit? While the batter is sticky, firmly press pea-sized cubes of raw potato onto it, then coat generously on all sides with panko breadcrumbs.

  4. 4

    Deep-fry in preheated vegetable oil (170°C) for about 3–5 minutes until evenly golden and crisp.

  5. 5

    Transfer to a paper towel, drizzle with sauces, and serve hot while the cheese stretches appetizingly.
