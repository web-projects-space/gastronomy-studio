Top 3 Sauces of Modern Western Gastronomy

⏱ 5 min 🍽 3 bases Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆

Three legendary bases adapted for everyday ingredients from your fridge: the gold standard of restaurant cooking right at home.

BASE SAUCES

  • 1. Velvety Hollandaise (ideal with poached eggs, asparagus, and fish):
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 150 g butter (82.5% fat)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of salt and pepper
  • 2. Classic Vinaigrette (all-purpose dressing):
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard + 1 tsp honey
  • 3. Yogurt Dip (great with vegetables and fried foods):
  • 150–200 g Greek yogurt
  • 1–2 garlic cloves
  • 3–4 sprigs of fresh dill
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice + oil

Chef's Tips

✦ Temperature for Hollandaise: The butter should be hot, while the yolks are warm. If it splits, add a spoonful of ice-cold water and whisk again.

✦ Vinaigrette Balance: If the dressing is too acidic, add a drop of honey; if it's bland, add a pinch of salt.

✦ Storage: Hollandaise is meant to be made for a single serving. Vinaigrette keeps well in a sealed jar for up to a week.

COOKING

  1. 1

    Hollandaise Sauce: Whisk the egg yolks with the lemon juice and seasonings over a double boiler. While whisking constantly, pour in the hot melted butter in a thin stream until thick and creamy. Serve immediately!

  2. 2

    Vinaigrette: In a small jar, combine the vinegar, salt, pepper, and honey until the crystals dissolve. Add the mustard and oil, then shake vigorously for 30 seconds until a stable emulsion forms.

  3. 3

    Yogurt Dip: Thoroughly mix all ingredients in a deep bowl. Refrigerate for 10–15 minutes to let the flavors meld.

  4. 4

    Serving: Pair the finished sauces with your favorite dishes and enjoy restaurant flavor at home!
