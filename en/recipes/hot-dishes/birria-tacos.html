Birria Tacos — Spicy Tacos in Broth

⏱ 2.5 hours 🍽 4 servings Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

Juicy, spiced beef braised in a peppery sauce and griddled with melted cheese inside a crisp tortilla. Served with rich consomé for dipping.

INGREDIENTS

  • 800 g beef brisket or chuck
  • 8–10 small corn tortillas
  • 200 g Mozzarella or Oaxaca cheese (finely grated)
  • 1 large onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 500 ml beef broth (or good-quality vegetable / bouillon cube)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp dried oregano and cumin
  • 1 cinnamon stick or 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon
  • Fresh cilantro, finely chopped red onion, and lime wedges for serving
  • Salt, black pepper, vegetable oil

Chef's Tips

✦ Don't be shy about brushing the tortilla generously with oil from the consomé. The spices meeting a hot skillet create that signature crisp texture and deep flavor people love in Birria.

✦ The beef should shred easily when pulled apart with two forks. Don't rush the process—long braising is what gives Birria its rich texture.

✦ Strain the remaining sauce into small bowls and top with fresh cilantro and onion. Dip each taco generously into the hot broth before taking a bite.

COOKING

  1. 1

    Cut the beef into large chunks, season with salt and pepper, and sear on all sides in a deep sauté pan over high heat until browned. Transfer the meat to a plate.

  2. 2

    In the same pan, sauté the sliced onion and garlic. Stir in the tomato paste, spices (oregano, cumin, cinnamon), vinegar, and beef broth. Return the meat to the pan, cover with a lid, and braise over low heat for 2–2.5 hours.

  3. 3

    Remove the tender meat and shred it into strands using two forks. Then gently skim the red oil layer from the surface of the pan with a spoon into a wide, shallow bowl or plate (for brushing the tortillas). Afterward, strain the remaining rich sauce (consomé) through a fine sieve and divide it among serving bowls.

  4. 4

    Heat a dry skillet. Using a brush (or pressing flat in a wide dish), coat a tortilla with the oil layer from the consomé and place it in the skillet. Spoon cheese and meat onto one half. Fold the tortilla in half and cook for 1–2 minutes per side until crisp and the cheese is melted.

  5. 5

    Serve the hot tacos alongside a bowl of fragrant consomé, garnished with chopped onion, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime juice.
