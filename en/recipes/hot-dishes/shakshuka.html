Smoky Shakshuka with Feta

⏱ 20 minutes 🍽 3 servings Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆

A spiced Middle Eastern breakfast or a quick, hearty dinner.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 eggs
  • 400 g tomatoes in their own juice (or finely chopped fresh tomatoes)
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 100 g feta cheese
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp cumin
  • Olive oil or butter for frying
  • Salt, freshly ground black pepper, fresh cilantro or parsley

Chef's Tips

✦ Cook the eggs covered for no more than 4–5 minutes over moderate heat so the whites set while the yolks stay creamy and runny.

✦ Toast the cumin and smoked paprika in the skillet with the onion and garlic for 30–60 seconds: fat-soluble essential oils will quickly fill the sauce with a deep "smoky" aroma.

✦ Serve shakshuka straight from the skillet with warm pita, baguette, or ciabatta—they're ideal for scooping up the juicy tomato sauce.

COOKING

  1. 1

    Finely chop the onion and bell pepper. Heat olive oil or butter in a skillet and sauté the vegetables until soft (5–7 minutes).

  2. 2

    Mince the garlic and add it to the vegetables along with the cumin and smoked paprika. Cook for 30–60 seconds until fragrant and spicy.

  3. 3

    Pour in the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper to taste. Simmer the sauce (matbucha) over medium heat for 5–7 minutes until slightly thickened.

  4. 4

    Make small wells in the sauce with a spoon and gently crack an egg into each one. Cover with a lid and cook over low heat for 4–5 minutes until the egg whites turn white.

  5. 5

    Remove from heat, crumble the feta over top, and scatter generously with fresh cilantro or parsley. Serve hot straight from the skillet!
